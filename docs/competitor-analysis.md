# StyleIQ Competitor Analysis Framework

**Status:** Research framework — current competitor findings have not yet been verified or recorded.

## Objective

Compare fashion shopping experiences to identify customer problems and opportunities. Do not assume that a feature is absent because it is not visible in one browsing session.

## Competitor set to investigate

- Myntra
- AJIO
- Amazon Fashion
- Other relevant fashion retailers and virtual try-on products discovered during research

## Comparison dimensions

| Dimension | What to inspect | Evidence to capture |
|---|---|---|
| Product imagery | Number and type of views | Screenshots and product examples |
| Size guidance | Size chart, body measurements, fit notes | Category and brand examples |
| Reviews | Fit comments, review photos, filters | Review UX and coverage |
| Virtual try-on | Availability, categories, workflow | Tested product and date |
| Personalization | Feed, recommendations, style preferences | New vs returning user |
| Inventory | Size and location availability | Product-page states |
| Returns | Policy clarity and reason capture | Public policy and checkout flow |
| Accessibility | Units, language, usability | Observed issues |
| Privacy | Photo consent and deletion controls | Policy and UI evidence |

## Research protocol

1. Record date, region, device, category, and account state.
2. Test more than one product and brand.
3. Separate features observed directly from claims found in marketing or help pages.
4. Save public source links and screenshots where permitted.
5. Note differences by product category and user state.
6. Re-check dynamic features before presenting findings.

## Opportunity scoring

Score each validated problem on:
- Customer pain severity
- Frequency in observed journeys
- Business impact
- Competitor differentiation
- Technical and data feasibility
- Privacy and trust risk

Do not assign a high priority solely because a feature looks innovative.
